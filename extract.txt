ailable some time in 1997. Q: What's a ".GIF"? A: GIF stands for "Graphics Interchange Format" and is a common format in which pictures are stored for display on a computer screen; when someone on soc.singles mentions something that someone else would really like to have seen, the latter person may jokingly ask for a "GIF"--but even if you're asked for one, don't even think of posting it to soc.singles, since they tend to be huge.
